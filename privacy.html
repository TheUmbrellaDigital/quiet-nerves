Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you visit this Site. By using the Site you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: This Site does not contain order forms. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you include in your message.

Information collected automatically: When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies, including your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, time spent on pages, video viewing activity, and the links you click.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We and our partners use cookies, pixels, and scripts to operate the Site, measure advertising performance, and attribute sales. These include:

  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel — measures advertising effectiveness and may be used to show you relevant ads on Meta platforms. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • UTMify — tracks campaign parameters (UTMs) and attributes conversions to traffic sources.
  • BuyGoods tracking — the retailer's affiliate and conversion tracking, which links your visit to any purchase you complete on the BuyGoods checkout.
  • Video player analytics — measures how the video on this page is viewed.
  • Cloudflare — the hosting provider, which may collect performance and security data.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how the Site functions.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site;
  • To measure and improve advertising and marketing campaigns;
  • To attribute purchases to the correct referral source;
  • To respond to your inquiries;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Purchases

All purchases are processed by BuyGoods on its own secure checkout. Payment details, shipping addresses, and order information are collected and handled by BuyGoods under its own Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the Site and our marketing (such as those listed above), with the product retailer for order fulfillment and attribution, and where required by law.

6. Data Retention

Automatically collected data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to opt out of certain processing. Residents of California and other U.S. states, and residents of the EU/UK, may have additional rights under applicable law. To exercise these rights, email support@quietnerves.com.

To opt out of interest-based advertising from Meta, adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to persons under 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: support@quietnerves.com.
